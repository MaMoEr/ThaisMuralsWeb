About me.

Hi, I’m Thais Eriksson, an artist and muralist in Stockholm. I paint walls by hand, with a brush and without stencils. Every project starts with a conversation about the room and ends with a wall that feels like yours.

From conversation to wall

How it works.

  1. Conversation

    You tell me about the room and send a photo of the wall.

  2. Sketch

    I make a first proposal, and the sketch is included.

  3. Approval

    We adjust the motif and colours until you’re happy.

  4. Painting

    I paint the wall by hand, on site at your place.

  5. Done

    You get a one-of-a-kind wall to live with for years.
